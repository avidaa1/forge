Papiamentu Quick Reference

Verified core orthography, greetings, and proverbs from the Papiamentu di Kòrsou system — the same reference standard used across the AVIDAA Papiamentu archive. No signup, no download, no marketplace hop.

Greetings
Bon bini (welcome) · Bon dia (good morning) · Bon tardi (good afternoon) · Bon nochi (good night) · Kon ta bai? (how are you?) · Mashá danki (thank you very much) · Di nada (you're welcome)

Core spelling rules
Phonemic system: k/s not c (kas not casa, sinku not cinco). Word-initial diphthongs shift i→y and u→w (yabi not iabi, wowo not uowo). Four digraphs: ch, dj, sh, zj.

A proverb
"Awa ketu sa gaña hende." — Still water deceives people.

About Forge

FILE 002

FORGE is the storefront wing of AVIDAA — a catalog, not a cashier. Nothing here processes a card, stores a payment detail, or takes a checkout step. Every crate hands off to its own marketplace listing, where the platform handles payment, tax, and delivery under its own terms. This keeps AVIDAA a pure knowledge-and-build surface, and keeps every transaction under a licensed platform's own compliance.
